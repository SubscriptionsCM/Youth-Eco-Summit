# Youth Eco Summit — Competition entry flow review

Friday, 9 October 2026. English translation of the supplied meeting summary.

The opening competition-entry screen was confusing. The agreed flow is: student or educator, competition, then individual or team where teams are permitted. Student submissions must include guardian contacts for every participating student.

## Entry flow issues

- The first screen after “Submit your entries” has too many buttons.
- “Register as a student” and “Already registered” are unclear and need simpler wording.
- Continuing a saved entry must be clearly identified: students may fill in details now and upload their work later.
- Schools are in a busy period before the holidays and are repeatedly calling about website changes.
- The later steps work smoothly; the opening screen needs to be simpler.

## Entry flow and contact details

- Ask whether the submitter is a student or an educator first, followed by the competition, then individual or team where allowed.
- When a student submits without an educator, collect every team member’s name, guardian email and guardian phone number.
- Pankaj’s concern is that participant contact details are not being captured.
- Selecting a school ID must automatically fill the school name and branch.
- The backend must identify student-submitted entries and entries submitted by educators on behalf of a school.

## Language and hosting

- Replace the unregistered-school warning with practical guidance: ask an educator to register the school, or register the school first.
- A Hostinger plan is needed. The buyer and payment method have not been decided.

## Decisions

- Educator submissions do not collect guardian details. Collect participating student names, classes and email addresses instead.
- Use “educator” in place of “teacher”.
- A student may enter several competitions, one entry at a time.
