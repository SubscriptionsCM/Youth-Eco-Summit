On 6 October 2026 the user confirmed student registrations and competition submissions are open and close on 5 November. School and teacher registration remain available after the cutoff. Each format includes class-specific topics, concise prompts, prescribed suggested-reading links, file roles and limits, individual/group limits, full score criteria and conditional recognition opportunities. Writing requires title/introduction/body/conclusion/references, author byline and typed work. Only reference hyperlinks are accepted; optional infographics are allowed for open letters/blogs and case studies.
